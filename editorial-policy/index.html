Editorial and sources policy

Last updated 19 Nov 2026.

This is not legal advice. This is not medical advice. This is not insurance advice. The pages are general information so you can read the federal rules before you talk with Social Security, Medicare, a SHIP counselor, or your state.

What we will publish

  • Rules and dollar amounts that we read on Medicare.gov, CMS.gov, SSA.gov, shiphelp.org, a state insurance department, or a state Medicaid agency.
  • The year the official page attaches to a dollar amount. A 2026 figure stays on a 2026 page. A 2027 figure stays on a 2027 page.
  • A last checked date, the source title, and the source URL on every guide and every state page.
  • The name and phone of a SHIP program or Medicaid agency only when the official directory prints them.

What we will not publish

  • A premium, copay, or plan name we did not read on an official page. Private plan prices are left to Medicare.gov and SHIP.
  • A recommendation to buy, drop, or switch a specific plan.
  • A figure for a later year on an earlier year page. The Extra Help page shows some copays under a 2027 heading; those stay off the 2026 Extra Help explainer until we publish a matching year page.
  • A state insurance department link we could not open. Ten states are missing that link on purpose.
  • Anything presented as an official Medicare communication. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. government or the federal Medicare program.

How a page is corrected

Email the address on the contact page with the URL, the sentence, and the official page that disagrees. If we confirm the error, we change the page and we keep the last checked date honest. We do not backdate a page to look freshly updated when the words did not change.

Dates

datePublished on the first version of these pages is 2026-11-16, the day the sources were read. It is not the clock time of a later rebuild. dateModified changes only when the words change.

Independence

No insurer pays for a mention. There are no affiliate links. An AdSense account tag may be present for domain verification before any ad units appear. See the advertising disclosure.
