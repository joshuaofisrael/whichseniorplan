What Original Medicare does not cover

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Short version: Original Medicare does not pay for everything. If Part A and Part B do not cover an item, you pay unless other coverage pays, or you are in a Medicare Advantage, Medicare Cost, or PACE plan that covers it.

Medicare.gov lists these examples of what Original Medicare does not cover:

  • Eye exams for prescription eyeglasses
  • Non medical long term care
  • Cosmetic surgery
  • Massage therapy
  • Routine physical exams
  • Hearing aids and exams for fitting them
  • Concierge care, also called retainer based or boutique medicine
  • Services from a doctor who has opted out of Medicare, except in an emergency or urgent need
  • Most dental care, including routine cleanings, tooth extractions, and dentures

The same page says Original Medicare may still pay for some dental services that are closely related to other covered care. It also says some Advantage, Cost, and PACE plans cover extra vision, hearing, or dental services. That is a feature of those plans, not a promise that every plan includes them.

A Medigap policy helps pay Original Medicare cost sharing. It does not turn an uncovered service into a covered one. Part D covers outpatient drugs that are on the plan formulary, not the gaps in this list.

Questions people ask

Does Medicare pay for a nursing home stay that is only custodial?

Medicare.gov lists non medical long term care among the services Original Medicare does not cover. A skilled nursing facility stay under Part A rules is different from custodial long term care.

Will Medigap pay for dental cleanings?

No. Medigap helps with costs Original Medicare already covers. It does not add routine dental coverage.

Can a private plan cover any of this?

Medicare.gov says some Medicare Advantage, Medicare Cost, and PACE plans cover extra vision, hearing, or dental services. You have to read that plan.
