Free SHIP counseling

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Short version: SHIP is the State Health Insurance Assistance Program. Counselors help you compare Medicare choices at no charge. They do not sell plans. The national number published by shiphelp.org is 1-877-839-2675. Your state uses its own program name and phone.

shiphelp.org says the national technical assistance center does not itself give individual counseling. You call the local program or use the SHIP locator. The local name is not always "SHIP." The same site gives HICAP, SHIIP, SHINE, and VICAP as examples. California uses HICAP. Florida uses SHINE. New York uses HIICAP. The state pages use the name and phone printed on that state's shiphelp.org page on 16 Nov 2026.

A counselor can explain enrollment deadlines, Medigap letters, Advantage and drug plans, and help with Medicare Savings Programs and Extra Help. Bring your questions. You do not give our site your Medicare number, and we have no form that sends your information to an insurance agent.

For federal questions, Medicare.gov lists 1-800-MEDICARE as 1-800-633-4227, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and TTY 1-877-486-2048. That line is not a substitute for the local SHIP if you want someone who works in your state.

Programs are funded through the U.S. Administration for Community Living. Which Senior Plan is not a SHIP and is not part of that program.

Questions people ask

Does SHIP charge a fee?

No. shiphelp.org describes SHIP as free Medicare counseling from the local program.

Will a SHIP counselor sell me a plan?

The program's role is counseling, not sales. Which Senior Plan also does not sell plans or share your contact details with agents.

What if the phone on a state page has changed?

Use the SHIP locator at shiphelp.org or call 1-877-839-2675. Each state page links the shiphelp.org page we checked and shows the date.
