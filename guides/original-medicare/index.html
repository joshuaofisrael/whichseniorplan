What Original Medicare is

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Short version: Original Medicare is Part A (hospital insurance) and Part B (medical insurance). It is the federal coverage itself, not a private plan. Drug coverage is a separate Part D plan. Medigap is optional extra insurance that helps pay your share.

Part A helps cover inpatient hospital care, skilled nursing facility care, hospice, and some home health care. Part B helps cover services from doctors and other providers, outpatient care, home health care, durable medical equipment, and many preventive services such as screenings, vaccines, and yearly wellness visits.

For 2026, Medicare.gov lists these standard Original Medicare costs. Most people pay no Part A premium because they paid Medicare taxes long enough, generally at least 10 years. If you have to buy Part A, the 2026 premium is $311 or $565 a month, depending on how long you or your spouse worked. The Part A hospital deductible is $1,736 for each benefit period. Days 1 through 60 in the hospital are $0 after that deductible. Days 61 through 90 are $434 a day. Lifetime reserve days are $868 a day. The standard Part B premium is $202.90 a month, the Part B deductible is $283 a year, and you usually pay 20 percent of the Medicare approved amount after the deductible. People with higher incomes pay more. Details and the income table are on the 2026 costs page.

Original Medicare does not, by itself, pay for most outpatient prescriptions. You join a separate Part D drug plan for that, or you get drug coverage through a Medicare Advantage plan instead of staying with Original Medicare. A Medigap policy can help pay deductibles and coinsurance, but only if you have Original Medicare.

Who gets it automatically

Medicare.gov says some people get Part A and Part B without a separate signup because they already get benefits from Social Security or the Railroad Retirement Board. That includes people who start those benefits at least 4 months before they turn 65, people who have received disability benefits for 24 months, and people with ALS when disability benefits start. If you live in Puerto Rico or outside the United States, Medicare.gov says you get Part A automatically and must sign up if you want Part B. Everyone else contacts Social Security to sign up. See the first enrollment period.

Questions people ask

Is Original Medicare a private insurance plan?

No. Part A and Part B are the federal coverage. Private companies sell Part D drug plans and Medigap policies that work with it, and private companies sell Medicare Advantage plans as the other way to get Medicare.

Does Original Medicare include prescriptions?

Not for most drugs you pick up at a pharmacy. You join a separate Medicare drug plan, or you choose a Medicare Advantage plan that includes drugs.

Is there a yearly cap on what I pay?

Medicare.gov says there is no yearly limit on what you pay out of pocket under Original Medicare unless you have other coverage, such as a Medigap policy, or you join a Medicare Advantage plan.
