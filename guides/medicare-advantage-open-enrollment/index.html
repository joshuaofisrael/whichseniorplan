Medicare Advantage open enrollment

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Short version: January 1 through March 31 is the Medicare Advantage open enrollment period. It is only for people already in a Medicare Advantage plan. You can switch to another Advantage plan, or drop it, return to Original Medicare, and join a drug plan. Coverage starts the first of the month after the plan gets your request.

Medicare.gov also says this kind of change is available within the first 3 months you have Medicare, if you are in an Advantage plan. It is not a window for someone who has only Original Medicare to join an Advantage plan. That change waits for the next fall open enrollment, unless a special enrollment period applies.

The same calendar dates, January 1 through March 31, are also the General Enrollment Period for Part A and Part B. That is a different rule for a different problem: people who missed their first Part B signup. Do not mix the two. One changes an Advantage plan you already have. The other signs you up for Part B, possibly with a penalty. Both are explained side by side on the enrollment overview.

If you drop an Advantage plan and return to Original Medicare, ask whether you have a right to buy a Medigap policy. That right is not automatic every winter. See Medigap open enrollment and guaranteed issue rights.

Questions people ask

Can I join my first Advantage plan in February?

Not under this window, unless you already have an Advantage plan or you are in your first 3 months of Medicare and already in a plan, or you have a separate special enrollment period.

How fast does a January switch start?

Medicare.gov says coverage starts the first of the month after the plan gets your request.

Is January 1 to March 31 also for Part B?

The dates match the General Enrollment Period, but that period is for signing up for Part B or premium Part A. It is a different use of the same months.
