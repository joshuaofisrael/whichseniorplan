Extra Help with drug costs

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Short version: Extra Help is a Medicare program that helps pay Part D premiums, deductibles, and other drug costs. In 2026, the Medicare.gov limits are $23,940 in income and $18,090 in resources for one person, and $32,460 and $36,100 for a married couple. You also pay no Part D late penalty while you have Extra Help.

Some people get Extra Help automatically: full benefit Medicaid, a Medicare Savings Program that pays the Part B premium, or Supplemental Security Income from Social Security. If that is you, Medicare sends a notice. It can also enroll you in a drug plan if you do not have one, and you can pick a different plan. If you still meet the limits, you keep Extra Help next year. Medicare mails a notice if you lose it or if you are moved to a different plan.

Everyone else can apply through Social Security. You can apply for Extra Help and a Medicare Savings Program at the same time. A SHIP counselor can help with the application at no charge.

The Extra Help page on Medicare.gov also shows copay amounts under a heading for 2027. We are not copying those later year figures onto a 2026 explainer. For 2026, the Medicare Savings Programs page says people who get Extra Help this way pay no more than $12.65 for each covered drug. If you think the pharmacy is charging the wrong amount, Medicare.gov says to contact the plan and that you can call 1-800-MEDICARE at 1-800-633-4227 (TTY 1-877-486-2048).

Extra Help is not available in Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, the Northern Mariana Islands, or American Samoa. Medicare.gov says other help exists there. People who have Medicaid or Extra Help may change drug coverage once a month, which most other people cannot do.

Questions people ask

Do I apply for Extra Help at the state Medicaid office?

Automatic Extra Help comes with full Medicaid, a Medicare Savings Program, or SSI. If you do not get it automatically, Medicare.gov says you apply, and Social Security takes that application. You can apply for both Extra Help and a Medicare Savings Program together.

Will Extra Help remove a Part D penalty?

Medicare.gov says you will not have to pay a Part D late enrollment penalty while you get Extra Help.

Why are 2027 copays not on this page?

The federal Extra Help page labels a set of copays as 2027 amounts. This site uses 2026 figures only where the official page labels them as 2026.
