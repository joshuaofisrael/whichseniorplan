Medicare costs in 2026

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 19 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

2027 figures are out. CMS published the 2027 Part A and Part B amounts on 17 Nov 2026. Use that page for next year. This page stays the 2026 table.

Short version: These are the 2026 amounts CMS and Medicare.gov published for Part A and Part B. They are not quotes for a Medicare Advantage, Part D, or Medigap policy. Those prices are set by the plan.

Part B

The standard premium is $202.90 a month. The deductible is $283 for the year. After the deductible you usually pay 20 percent of the Medicare approved amount for Part B services. The standard premium for immunosuppressive drug coverage only, for certain people whose full Medicare ended 36 months after a kidney transplant, is $121.60 a month.

Higher premiums based on income

CMS says about 8 percent of people with Part B pay an income related monthly adjustment. The 2026 full Part B amounts, using modified adjusted gross income, are below. The income is from the tax return Social Security uses, not a guess about this year's pay.

Married people who lived with a spouse and file a separate return pay a Part B total of $202.90 if income is $109,000 or less, $649.20 if income is above $109,000 and under $391,000, and $689.90 at $391,000 or more. The matching Part D add on amounts are $0, $83.30, and $91.00. The Part D amount is added to the plan premium. It is not the plan premium.

Help with these costs, if your income is limited, is a Medicare Savings Program run by your state. For outpatient drug rules in 2026, including the $2,100 out of pocket cap, see Part D. For free local counseling, use SHIP.

Questions people ask

Are these the prices of Medicare Advantage plans?

No. Advantage, Part D, and Medigap premiums are set by each company. The figures on this page are the federal Part A and Part B amounts for 2026.

Does everyone pay $202.90 for Part B?

That is the standard 2026 premium. People with higher incomes pay more, and a late enrollment penalty can add more. Some people pay less because a state Medicare Savings Program pays the premium.

Why does the Part A deductible say each benefit period?

CMS and Medicare.gov charge the hospital deductible once per benefit period, not once per calendar year. A new benefit period can mean another deductible.
