Original Medicare or Medicare Advantage

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Short version: Original Medicare is Part A and Part B from the federal program. Medicare Advantage is a private plan that provides those benefits. You use one path at a time. This page describes the difference. It does not tell you which path to take.

Medicare.gov describes two main ways to get coverage. With Original Medicare you can use any doctor or hospital that takes Medicare. You can join a separate drug plan for Part D. You can also buy a Medigap policy to help pay your share of the costs. There is no yearly limit on what you pay out of pocket under Original Medicare unless you have other coverage, such as Medigap, or you join a Medicare Advantage plan.

Medicare Advantage, also called Part C, is the other path. You still have Medicare, and you must keep paying the Part B premium. The private plan provides the Part A and Part B benefits, and most plans include drug coverage. You may have to use doctors in the plan network and get approval before some services or drugs. The plan sets its own copays and has a yearly limit on out of pocket costs. After you reach that limit, the plan pays the full cost of covered health services for the rest of the year. Many plans offer extra benefits Original Medicare does not cover, such as some vision, hearing, or dental care. Those extras are not the same in every plan.

Medigap works with Original Medicare. Medicare.gov says you can buy a Medigap policy only if you have Original Medicare. This site does not list private plan names or prices. Use the plan comparison tool on Medicare.gov, or a SHIP counselor, for plans sold where you live.

A simple way to sort the choice

  • Doctors: Original Medicare works with any provider that takes Medicare. An Advantage plan may use a network.
  • Drugs: Original Medicare needs a separate Part D plan if you want drug coverage. Most Advantage plans include drugs.
  • Your share of the bill: Original Medicare charges the Part B deductible and usually 20 percent coinsurance, with no yearly cap unless you add coverage. Advantage plans set copays and have a yearly out of pocket limit.
  • Extras: Routine dental, vision, and hearing are generally outside Original Medicare. Some Advantage plans add them. Read that plan.

When you can join or leave a plan is covered under enrollment periods. Nationwide 2026 dollar amounts are on the 2026 costs page.

Questions people ask

Can I have Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage at the same time?

No. Medicare.gov describes them as the two main ways to get Medicare coverage. You use one or the other. You can switch only during enrollment periods Medicare publishes.

Does Medicare Advantage replace Part A and Part B?

The plan provides your Part A and Part B benefits, but you stay in Medicare and you must keep paying the Part B premium.

Does this site recommend one path?

No. We explain the rules Medicare publishes. A SHIP counselor can help you compare plans where you live. We do not sell plans.
