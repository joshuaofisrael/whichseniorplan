Medicare and Medicaid together

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 21 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Full Medicaid is not the income table. The federal 2026 limits for QMB, SLMB, and QI are on the savings program page. Extra Help drug copays are on the Extra Help page. Losing Medicaid can open a special enrollment period that those pages do not describe.

Short version: Medicare is federal health insurance for people 65 and older and for some younger people with disabilities. Medicaid is a joint federal and state program. Medicare.gov says that if you qualify, you can have both. The state decides Medicaid eligibility. This page does not print a state's full Medicaid income limit, because those rules are not one national table.

Having both is often called being dual eligible. Full Medicaid can cover services Medicare does not, and it can lead to Extra Help with drugs. A Medicare Savings Program is narrower. QMB, SLMB, QI, and QDWI help with Medicare premiums and, for QMB, Medicare cost sharing. Medicare.gov, read 21 Nov 2026, still labels those income limits as 2026: $1,350, $1,616, and $1,816 a month for one person. They are not the same thing as full Medicaid, even though the state Medicaid agency usually takes the application.

Someone who has both programs and needs a nursing home level of care may also be able to join PACE in areas that offer it. The March 2026 Choosing a Medigap Policy guide describes PACE as medical, social, and long term care, plus drug coverage, for older adults who need nursing home services and can still live in the community. It is an optional Medicaid benefit, so it is not in every state.

Medigap is usually the wrong assumption

Two official sentences, read together, are easy to miss. The March 2026 guide says it is illegal for someone to sell you a Medigap policy if they know you have Medicaid, except in limited situations. The guide names the exception and does not list the situations. The Medicare.gov "When can I buy a Medigap policy?" tool, read 21 Nov 2026, says insurance companies generally cannot sell you a Medigap policy if you have Medicaid, even if you have both Medicare and Medicaid. We are not filling in the "limited situations" the guide left unnamed. A SHIP counselor or the state Medicaid office is the place to ask whether a supplement can be sold in your case. Do not assume a Medigap letter is available because you have Original Medicare.

The premium help that is designed for this overlap is the savings program, especially QMB, which Medicare.gov says stops providers from billing you for Medicare covered deductibles, coinsurance, and copays. That is a different mechanism from buying a private letter.

Where the state facts are, and where they are not

Medicaid.gov publishes a directory of state agencies, phones, and websites. Each state page repeats the agency name, the member phone numbers, and the link from that directory, checked 16 Nov 2026. If a line has changed, the directory controls. We still do not invent a state's income limit for full Medicaid.

If you lose Medicaid, Medicare.gov lists a special chance to sign up for Part B. On the coverage start page read 21 Nov 2026, a loss on or after January 1, 2023 opens a period that ends 6 months after Medicaid ends, using form CMS-10797. Coverage can begin the month after you sign up, or the date Medicaid ends, whichever you choose. The rest of that table is on the special enrollment page. People who have Medicaid or Extra Help may also change drug coverage once a month. Most other people cannot.

Questions people ask

Can I have Medicare and Medicaid at the same time?

Yes. Medicare.gov says that if you qualify, you can have both. The state decides the Medicaid side.

Is a Medicare Savings Program the same as Medicaid?

It is run through the state and QMB, SLMB, or QI can come with Extra Help, but those four programs help with Medicare costs. Full Medicaid is broader and uses state rules this site does not invent.

Can someone with Medicaid buy a Medigap policy?

The March 2026 CMS guide says a sale is illegal if the seller knows you have Medicaid, except in limited situations it does not list. The Medicare.gov tool read 21 Nov 2026 says companies generally cannot sell Medigap to someone with Medicaid. Ask SHIP or the state before you assume a policy is available.
