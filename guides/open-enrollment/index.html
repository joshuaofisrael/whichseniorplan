Open enrollment: October 15 to December 7

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 21 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

December 7 is not a Medigap deadline. This window changes Advantage and drug plans. The one time Medigap window is six months from Part B at 65 or older. A later right to buy certain letters is guaranteed issue, and the fall calendar does not create it. People already in an Advantage plan get another switch from January 1 to March 31.

Short version: October 15 through December 7 is the yearly open enrollment for Medicare Advantage and Medicare drug plans. Medicare.gov's joining a plan page, read 21 Nov 2026, says the new coverage starts January 1, and the plan has to have your request by December 7.

During those weeks you can join, drop, or switch to another Medicare Advantage plan, with or without drug coverage. You can move from Original Medicare to Medicare Advantage, or from Medicare Advantage back to Original Medicare. If you stay with Original Medicare, you can join, drop, or switch a standalone drug plan. You have to live in the plan's service area. If you live in another state for part of the year, Medicare.gov says to check whether that plan will cover you there before you join.

This is not a new Initial Enrollment Period for Part B. If you never signed up for Part B, the fall does not replace the General Enrollment Period or a special enrollment period. It is also not the one time Medigap open enrollment. Dropping an Advantage plan in the fall does not by itself give you a right to buy any Medigap letter without health questions. Those later rights are specific situations on the guaranteed issue page, with their own 63 day clocks.

How a fall request actually gets in

The joining a plan page lists three ways, and none of them is a form on this site. You can select Enroll for a plan at medicare.gov/plan-compare. You can contact the plan, by phone, on its site, or with a paper form the plan has to receive before December 7. Or you can call 1-800-MEDICARE at 1-800-633-4227 (TTY 1-877-486-2048). You need your Medicare number and your Part A and Part B start dates, which are on the Medicare card. To join an Advantage plan you need both parts. A standalone drug plan needs either one.

Before that call, Medicare.gov's own checklist is practical and it is not a recommendation of a company. Add the prescriptions you take so the cost estimate is about your drugs. Ask the doctors and the pharmacy whether they are in the plan's network. Read the premium, the deductible, and the yearly drug estimate. If you already have other health or drug coverage, talk to that benefits office before you change. A star rating on the federal tool is the plan's rating, not a promise about your doctors. We do not copy star ratings or premiums onto this site. The steps for reading a plan without a sales pitch are on how to compare plans.

People with Medicaid or Extra Help may be able to change drug coverage once a month, which is a different rule from this fall window. SHIP counselors are busiest now. The national line on shiphelp.org is 1-877-839-2675. Your state page has the local program. We do not ask for a Medicare number.

Questions people ask

When does a plan I join in the fall start?

January 1, if the plan gets your request by December 7. Medicare.gov's joining a plan page, read 21 Nov 2026, still says that.

Can I use this window to sign up for Part B for the first time?

No. This window changes Advantage and drug coverage. Part B signup uses the Initial Enrollment Period, a special enrollment period, or the General Enrollment Period from January 1 through March 31.

Does leaving an Advantage plan in the fall guarantee a Medigap policy?

No. Medigap open enrollment is a one time 6 month period. A guaranteed issue right is a listed situation, such as a plan leaving Medicare, not the October calendar by itself.
