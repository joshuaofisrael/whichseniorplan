Open enrollment: October 15 to December 7

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Short version: October 15 through December 7 is the yearly open enrollment for Medicare Advantage and Medicare drug plans. If the plan gets your request by December 7, the new coverage starts January 1.

Medicare.gov says that during this period you can join, drop, or switch to another Medicare Advantage plan with or without drug coverage. You can move from Original Medicare to Medicare Advantage, or from Medicare Advantage back to Original Medicare. If you are in Original Medicare, you can join, drop, or switch a standalone drug plan.

This is not a new Initial Enrollment Period for Part B. If you never signed up for Part B, this fall window does not replace the General Enrollment Period or a special enrollment period. It is also not the one time Medigap open enrollment. Dropping an Advantage plan in the fall does not by itself give you a fresh right to buy any Medigap letter without health questions.

SHIP counselors are busiest in this season. The national SHIP line published by shiphelp.org is 1-877-839-2675, and 1-800-MEDICARE is 1-800-633-4227 (TTY 1-877-486-2048). Your state page lists the local program. We do not run a plan picker and we do not ask for your Medicare number.

Questions people ask

When does a plan I join in the fall start?

January 1, if the plan gets your request by December 7.

Can I use this window to sign up for Part B for the first time?

No. This window changes Advantage and drug coverage. Part B signup uses the Initial Enrollment Period, a special enrollment period, or the General Enrollment Period from January 1 through March 31.

Is this the same as Medigap open enrollment?

No. Medigap open enrollment is a one time 6 month period that starts when you are 65 or older and you have Part B.
