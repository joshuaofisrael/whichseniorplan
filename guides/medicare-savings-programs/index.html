Medicare Savings Programs

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Short version: Medicare Savings Programs help pay Medicare premiums and, for one program, deductibles and coinsurance. Your state runs them. The 2026 federal limits are below. Alaska and Hawaii use slightly higher income limits, and many states let people qualify with more income or resources than this table.

ProgramWhat it helps payMonthly income, single / coupleResources, single / couple
QMBPart A and Part B premiums, and deductibles, coinsurance, and copays for Medicare covered services$1,350 / $1,824$9,950 / $14,910
SLMBPart B premium. You need Part A and Part B$1,616 / $2,184$9,950 / $14,910
QIPart B premium. You need Part A and Part B. You apply every year$1,816 / $2,455$9,950 / $14,910
QDWIPart A premium only, for some working people with a disability who lost premium free Part A$5,405 / $7,299$4,000 / $6,000

If you qualify for QMB, SLMB, or QI, you also get Extra Help with drug costs. Medicare.gov says you will pay no more than $12.65 in 2026 for each drug your plan covers. QMB also means Medicare providers are not allowed to bill you for Medicare covered services, including deductibles and coinsurance. A small Medicaid copay can still apply.

QI has extra rules. You must apply every year. States approve applications first come, first served, with priority for people who had QI the year before. QI is only for people who do not qualify for other Medicaid coverage.

Apply through the state Medicaid agency, even if you think you are over the federal limits. Each state page names the agency Medicaid.gov lists and the phone numbers on that directory. Limits can change, and this table is the 2026 federal set.

Questions people ask

Do these programs replace Medicare?

No. They help pay Medicare costs. You still have Medicare. The state decides which program you qualify for.

What if my income is a little over the table?

Apply anyway. Medicare.gov says you may qualify in your state even if income or resources are higher than the federal limits, and that Alaska and Hawaii use slightly higher income limits.

Does QMB stop doctor bills?

For services and items Medicare covers, Medicare.gov says providers are not allowed to bill a QMB enrollee for the deductible, coinsurance, or copay. Show both cards.
