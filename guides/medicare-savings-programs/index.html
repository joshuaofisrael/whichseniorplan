Medicare Savings Program income limits in 2026

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 21 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

These limits are not Extra Help, and they are not IRMAA. Extra Help is the drug program. A savings program that pays the Part B premium can bring Extra Help with it. People above these incomes have the opposite problem: the 2027 IRMAA brackets add to the Part B premium instead of paying it. The premium itself is on the 2026 costs page.

Short version: Medicare.gov, read 21 Nov 2026, still labels the Medicare Savings Program income and resource table as 2026. For one person the monthly income limits are $1,350 for QMB, $1,616 for SLMB, and $1,816 for QI. A couple is $1,824, $2,184, and $2,455. Resource limits for those three are $9,950 and $14,910. This page does not print 2027 limits. Medicare.gov has not put a 2027 table on that page.

The programs help pay Medicare costs. They do not replace Medicare. Your state Medicaid agency takes the application and decides which program fits. Each state page names the agency and the phone numbers from the Medicaid.gov directory. Apply there even if the numbers below look too low for your household.

The asterisk on every income cell of that Medicare.gov table says the income limits are slightly higher in Alaska and Hawaii. The higher dollar amounts are not in the table. This page does not invent them. The same footnote says you may qualify in other states even if income or resources are higher than these federal limits, because some states do not count certain types or amounts of income or resources. Ask the state. Do not treat a number a few dollars over the line as a no.

What each program pays, and what it does not

QMB is the wide one. It can pay the Part A premium if you do not have premium free Part A, the Part B premium, and the deductibles, coinsurance, and copays for services and items Medicare covers. Medicare.gov says providers are not allowed to bill a QMB enrollee for those Medicare covered services. A small Medicaid copay can still show up. Show the Medicare card and the Medicaid or QMB card at each visit. If you have Original Medicare, a Medicare Summary Notice can also show that you are in QMB.

SLMB and QI pay the Part B premium only. They do not, on Medicare.gov's description, pay the Part A premium or the doctor bills QMB stops. Both require Part A and Part B. The standard 2026 Part B premium those programs can cover is $202.90 a month, on the 2026 costs page. The program pays that premium. It is not a check for some other amount, and it is not the Part B give back some Advantage plans advertise.

QI has three extra rules, all on the same Medicare.gov page. You apply every year to stay in it. States approve applications first come, first served, and people who had QI the year before get priority. QI is only for people who do not qualify for other Medicaid coverage. You might still qualify for a different savings program if QI is closed.

QDWI is the odd row. The income limits are much higher and the resource limits are much lower: $4,000 for one person and $6,000 for a couple, not the $9,950 and $14,910 used for QMB, SLMB, and QI. Medicare.gov limits it to people who have a disability, are working, and lost Social Security disability benefits and premium free Part A because they returned to work. It pays the Part A premium only. The 2026 Part A premiums, $311 or $565 depending on work quarters, are on the costs page. QDWI's section on the savings program page does not say it includes Extra Help. The QMB, SLMB, and QI sections do.

Extra Help comes with three of the four

If you qualify for QMB, SLMB, or QI, Medicare.gov says you also get Extra Help with drug costs, and that you pay no more than $12.65 in 2026 for each drug the plan covers. That $12.65 line is a 2026 sentence on the savings program page. It is not the 2027 Extra Help copay schedule, which the Extra Help page keeps separate because Medicare.gov put those copays under a 2027 heading.

Full Medicaid is broader than these four programs. Someone can have both Medicare and Medicaid without being in QMB. The difference is on the Medicare and Medicaid page. A SHIP counselor can sit with the application. This site has no form and does not send your name to an agency.

Questions people ask

Are the Medicare Savings Program limits still the 2026 numbers?

Yes, on the Medicare.gov page read 21 Nov 2026. The income and resource tables are labeled 2026. This page does not guess a 2027 table.

What if my income is a little over the table?

Apply anyway. Medicare.gov says Alaska and Hawaii use slightly higher income limits, and that you may qualify in your state even if income or resources are higher than the federal figures, because some income and resources are not counted.

Does QMB stop doctor bills?

For services and items Medicare covers, Medicare.gov says providers are not allowed to bill a QMB enrollee for the deductible, coinsurance, or copay. A small Medicaid copay can still apply. Show both cards.
