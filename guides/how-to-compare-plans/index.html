How to compare plans without a sales pitch

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Short version: Compare plans on Medicare.gov or with your state SHIP program. Have your drug list, your doctors, and the enrollment window you are in. This website will not rank plans or ask for your phone number.

Use the federal tool

Medicare.gov runs the plan comparison tool at medicare.gov/plan-compare. It asks where you live because Advantage and drug plans are local. It can show premiums, deductibles, drug costs, and star ratings that we are not copying onto these pages. Those figures change. A copied list would go stale, and a made up list would be worse.

Or sit with a counselor

SHIP counseling is free and local. The counselor does not work for an insurance company. The national line is 1-877-839-2675, and every state page has the local number from shiphelp.org. That is the right place for "which plan should I pick," which this site will not answer.

What to have ready

  • Your ZIP code and whether you have Medicaid or Extra Help
  • The prescriptions you take, and the dose
  • The doctors and hospitals you want to keep
  • Which enrollment period you are actually in

Then separate the decision into the path (Original Medicare plus Part D, with or without Medigap, or Medicare Advantage) and the specific contract. The comparison page is only the first split. A star rating is not a promise about your drugs or your doctors.

Do not send your Medicare number, Social Security number, or bank details through this site. We have no quote form. Anyone who calls you because you "requested Medicare quotes" did not get your name from us.

Questions people ask

Why are there no plan names on this site?

Plan names, premiums, and formularies change, and listing them would look like a recommendation. Medicare.gov and SHIP are the places that can show current plans.

Can I compare Medigap here?

You can compare what each letter covers on our Medigap page. Prices are not listed. Your state insurance department and SHIP can show policies sold to you.

Is the Plan Finder a government site?

Yes. medicare.gov/plan-compare is on the federal Medicare site. Which Senior Plan is not that site and is not endorsed by it.
