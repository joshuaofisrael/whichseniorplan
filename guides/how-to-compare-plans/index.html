How to compare plans without a sales pitch

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 21 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Advantage plans and Medigap letters are not the same kind of comparison. A letter chart is on Medigap letters: the same letter has the same basic benefits. An Advantage plan is a local contract. The deadline you are in is on enrollment periods. Help with the premium is on the 2026 savings program limits.

Short version: Compare Medicare Advantage and drug plans on Medicare.gov, or with a SHIP counselor. The joining a plan page, read 21 Nov 2026, says to add your drugs, ask whether your doctors are in network, and read the premium, deductible, and yearly drug estimate. This website will not rank a company or ask for your phone number.

Start with the path, then the contract

The first split is still Original Medicare or Medicare Advantage. Original Medicare plus a separate drug plan, with or without a Medigap policy, is one path. An Advantage plan that delivers Part A and Part B, usually with drugs, is the other. You use one path at a time. A Medigap letter does not sit inside an Advantage plan. The March 2026 Choosing a Medigap Policy guide says the premium is the only difference between policies with the same letter sold by different companies, and it tells shoppers to compare the same letter with the same letter. That sentence is about Medigap. It is not true of Advantage plans, which are not standardized by letter.

Federal dollar amounts that do not depend on a private contract are already on this site, with the year attached. The 2026 costs page and the 2027 costs page are those baselines. A plan premium on Medicare.gov is a different number. We do not copy it, because it changes by county and by year, and a copied list would go stale.

What the federal tool asks you to check

Medicare.gov runs the comparison at medicare.gov/plan-compare. It asks where you live because Advantage and drug plans are local. The joining a plan page, read 21 Nov 2026, tells you to add every prescription you take regularly so the monthly and yearly estimate is about your drugs, not a generic basket. It also says to ask your doctors and pharmacies if they are in the plan's network, to review premiums, deductibles, and the drug estimate, and to talk to any other insurer before you change. If you live in another state for part of the year, check whether the plan covers you there. A star rating on that tool is Medicare's rating of the plan. It is not a promise about your prescriptions. We do not reprint star ratings.

You generally need Part A and Part B to join an Advantage plan, and either part to join a standalone drug plan. You have to live in the service area, and you need the Medicare number and the Part A and Part B start dates from the card. Once you have chosen, Medicare.gov lists three ways to join: select Enroll on plan-compare, contact the plan, or call 1-800-MEDICARE at 1-800-633-4227 (TTY 1-877-486-2048). The plan has to get a paper request before the enrollment period ends. Which period you are in is not optional. Fall open enrollment, the January Advantage window, and a special situation do different jobs. Read the deadlines before you treat a tool result as something you can buy today.

Medigap prices are a different search

Plan Finder is for Advantage and drug plans. Medigap prices are not a letter chart. The letters page shows what each letter covers in 2026. Whether a company must sell you that letter is the 6 month window or a guaranteed issue right. The March 2026 guide tells shoppers to ask the company how it sets the price, community, issue age, or attained age, and whether the premium rose in the last 3 years. Those answers are company specific. A SHIP counselor can use a state rate guide if the state publishes one. The national SHIP line on shiphelp.org is 1-877-839-2675. Counselors are not connected to an insurance company. That is the place for "which plan should I pick." This site will not answer it.

Do not send your Medicare number, Social Security number, or bank details through this site. We have no quote form. Anyone who calls you because you "requested Medicare quotes" did not get your name from us.

Questions people ask

Why are there no plan names on this site?

Plan names, premiums, and formularies change, and listing them would look like a recommendation. Medicare.gov and SHIP are the places that can show current plans.

Can I compare Medigap here?

You can compare what each letter covers on the letters page. The March 2026 CMS guide says the price is the only difference between the same letter at different companies. Those prices are not listed here.

Is the Plan Finder a government site?

Yes. medicare.gov/plan-compare is on the federal Medicare site. Which Senior Plan is not that site and is not endorsed by it.
