What Medicare covers

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Short version: Part A helps with inpatient hospital care, skilled nursing facility care, hospice, and some home health care. Part B helps with doctor services, outpatient care, durable medical equipment, and many preventive services. A specific item can still be uncovered.

Medicare.gov says coverage of a test, item, or service depends on three things: federal and state laws, national coverage decisions, and local coverage decisions by the companies that process claims in each state. Those companies decide whether something is medically necessary in their area. The practical step Medicare.gov gives is to ask the doctor whether Medicare will cover the service, and to check that item on Medicare.gov.

Original Medicare is not a list we can safely shorten into "everything medically necessary." The federal site keeps an item by item list for that reason. We link it rather than retype it. Things Original Medicare generally does not cover are on the not covered page, including non medical long term care, routine hearing aids, and most dental care.

A Medicare Advantage plan has to cover what Original Medicare covers, and it may add extras. It can also use networks and prior approval. Confirm the item with that plan, not with a general article.

Questions people ask

Does Medicare cover every service my doctor orders?

Not automatically. Medicare.gov says coverage also depends on national and local decisions about medical necessity.

Where do I check one test or piece of equipment?

On Medicare.gov, in the coverage search, and with the doctor who would order it.

Does Advantage cover the same services?

Medicare.gov says to ask whether your Medicare Advantage plan covers the same items and services. Plans can also offer extra benefits.
