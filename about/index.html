About Which Senior Plan

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Which Senior Plan is an independent explainer of senior health plan choices in the United States. The question we answer is which kind of coverage exists, what the federal rules say, and who to call in your state.

What we will not do

We do not sell insurance, quote a premium, rank a company, or collect a form that an agent could use as a lead. We do not put the Medicare name in our logo, and we are not a government site.

Where the facts come from

Medicare.gov, CMS, shiphelp.org, Medicaid.gov, and state insurance departments we could open. The rules for what we will print are on the editorial and sources policy.

See a mistake? Write to us and include the page and the official source.

Which Senior Plan explains senior health plan choices in plain English, using public federal and state pages. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. government or the federal Medicare program. We do not sell plans.
