Medicare help in California: HICAP and the birthday rule

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 20 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Who to call in California: California Health Insurance Counseling and Advocacy Program (HICAP), 1-800-434-0222. For help paying premiums, Department of Health Care Services runs the state Medicaid and Medicare Savings Programs. We do not list private plan names or premiums.

Medigap in this state

In California, Medigap policies use the standardized letters Medicare.gov publishes for most states: A, B, C, D, F, G, K, L, M, and N. The same letter has the same basic benefits at every company. The premium is not standardized, and this page does not list premiums or company names.

What HICAP will sit down and do

California's counseling program is HICAP, the Health Insurance Counseling and Advocacy Program, inside the California Department of Aging. The department's page says the counseling is free and confidential, and that it is one to one. It covers Medicare questions, long term care insurance, and planning for long term care. It also says HICAP can help with an appeal or a denial, and can provide legal assistance or a legal referral on Medicare or long term care insurance problems. That is the state program talking about its own service. Which Senior Plan does not tell you which contract to buy.

The same page limits who the counseling is for: people 65 or older who are eligible for Medicare, people under 65 with a disability who are eligible for Medicare, and people who will be eligible soon. The phone printed on shiphelp.org, and repeated by the Department of Aging, is 1-800-434-0222. You can also pick a county office from the department's site. HICAP is not an insurance company, and a counselor is not a plan salesperson.

Two different Medigap clocks

Insurance Code section 10192.11 sets both. The first is the familiar 6 month window. It begins on the first day of the first month in which the person is both 65 or older and enrolled in Part B. During that window an issuer may not deny a policy, condition it, or charge more because of health status, claims experience, medical care, or a medical condition. Every Medicare supplement policy the company currently sells has to be available to those applicants.

The second clock is the birthday rule, in subdivision (h). A person who already has Medicare supplement coverage gets an annual open enrollment of 60 days or more, starting on the birthday. In that window the person may buy a policy with benefits equal to or less than the policy they already have. The issuer may not deny the policy or price it from health, claims, or medical condition, as long as the person is covered by another Medicare supplement policy when the window opens. The company has to send notice at least 30 days, and no more than 60 days, before the window, and it has to put the notice on any letter about a benefit change or a premium change.

Read that limit twice. The birthday window is not a pass to move from a lean letter to a richer one without health questions. Equal or lesser benefits is the statute's line. It is also not the fall October 15 to December 7 period, which changes Advantage and drug plans and does not reopen Medigap underwriting. The letter benefits themselves are the federal chart on the letters page. Premiums are not printed here. Ask HICAP or the plan, and compare the current contracts on Medicare.gov.

The birthday rule is not national. Florida uses issue age pricing and a one time window. New York accepts Medigap applications all year. Texas gives six months from the Part B start date. Federal timing is on Medigap open enrollment.

What is the same in every state

Part A and Part B rules, the 2026 federal premiums on our costs page, and the enrollment deadlines come from federal law. Advantage and drug plans that are actually sold here are local. Compare them on Medicare.gov or with the counseling program above. Read Original Medicare or Medicare Advantage before you pick a path.

Questions people ask

Who offers free Medicare counseling in California?

California Health Insurance Counseling and Advocacy Program (HICAP) is the program listed on shiphelp.org. The number on that page is 1-800-434-0222.

Where do I apply for a Medicare Savings Program in California?

Medicaid.gov lists Department of Health Care Services. Applications go to the state, not to this website.

Does California use the standard Medigap letters?

Yes. Medicare.gov standardizes letters A, B, C, D, F, G, K, L, M, and N in this state. Prices still differ by company.

What does the California birthday rule allow?

Insurance Code section 10192.11 gives someone who already has a Medicare supplement policy 60 days or more, starting on the birthday, to buy another policy with equal or lesser benefits. The issuer cannot use health to deny that policy or set the price.

Is the birthday rule the same as October open enrollment?

No. Fall open enrollment changes Medicare Advantage and Part D plans. The birthday rule is a state Medigap window, and only for a policy that is not richer than the one you already have.
