Medicare help in New York: year round Medigap

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 20 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Who to call in New York: Health Insurance Information, Counseling and Assistance (HIICAP), 1-800-701-0501. For help paying premiums, New York State Department of Health runs the state Medicaid and Medicare Savings Programs. We do not list private plan names or premiums.

Medigap in this state

In New York, Medigap policies use the standardized letters Medicare.gov publishes for most states: A, B, C, D, F, G, K, L, M, and N. The same letter has the same basic benefits at every company. The premium is not standardized, and this page does not list premiums or company names.

We did not confirm a state insurance department homepage on this pass, so we are not guessing a web address. A SHIP counselor can point you to the office that regulates Medigap.

Applications do not stop after six months

The New York Department of Financial Services says state law and regulation require any insurer that writes Medigap to accept a Medicare enrollee's application at any time of year. The insurer may not deny the policy, and may not set the premium apart, because of health status, claims experience, medical condition, or because the person is in treatment. A policy sold to a group is different: only members of that group can buy it.

DFS says this open enrollment and the portability rules cover people who have Medicare because of age or because of disability, and people with end stage renal disease. That is wider than the federal 6 month window, which is tied to age 65 and the start of Part B. New York did not copy that calendar. HIICAP, the counseling program, is still the right place to walk through a specific policy. The number on the shiphelp.org listing is 1-800-701-0501. This page does not rank letters or companies.

One rate for the policy form

Insurance Law section 4317 community rates Medicare supplemental coverage. For that coverage, the premium for everyone on a given policy form is the same, based on the experience of the pool covered by that form, without regard to age, sex, health status, tobacco use, or occupation. The superintendent can allow separate community rates for geographic regions, down to a single county, if the region is approved in the rate filing. DFS publishes a carrier list and premium comparison tables, including a premium table it marked updated 1 Oct 2026. We are not copying those dollars. They change, and a copied price would be a plan quote.

Companies do not have to sell every letter. DFS says that for policies sold on or after 1 Jan 2020, an insurer offers plans A and B and either D or G. Not every company offers the rest. Plans C and F remain closed to people who became eligible on or after 1 Jan 2020, which is the federal rule on our letters page. People who already had C or F can keep renewing them.

Waiting periods, and a product that is not for sale

A Medigap policy may wait up to 6 months before it covers a pre existing condition. DFS defines that as a condition for which a physician gave or recommended treatment in the 6 months before the effective date. The insurer has to shorten the wait by days the person already had creditable coverage, if any break was not more than 63 days. Creditable coverage on the DFS list includes a group health plan, Medicaid, TRICARE, the Federal Employees Health Benefits Program, and a prior Medicare supplement, Medicare Select, or Medicare Advantage plan. Credit for time on Medicare itself is required only if the application arrives before or during the 6 month period that starts when the person is both 65 or older and enrolled in Part B.

Medicare Select, the network version of Medigap, is a blank row in New York right now. DFS says no insurer is currently offering Medicare Select in the state. If that changes, the DFS page is the place to check, not a recycled sentence on this site. Advantage plans are federal. DFS tells New Yorkers to use Medicare.gov for those contracts, which is the same instruction on our comparison guide.

Most states close the window. Texas gives six months from the Part B start. California adds a birthday window for an equal or lesser policy. Florida locks the rate to the age at purchase. The federal one time window is on Medigap open enrollment.

What is the same in every state

Part A and Part B rules, the 2026 federal premiums on our costs page, and the enrollment deadlines come from federal law. Advantage and drug plans that are actually sold here are local. Compare them on Medicare.gov or with the counseling program above. Read Original Medicare or Medicare Advantage before you pick a path.

Questions people ask

Who offers free Medicare counseling in New York?

Health Insurance Information, Counseling and Assistance (HIICAP) is the program listed on shiphelp.org. The number on that page is 1-800-701-0501.

Where do I apply for a Medicare Savings Program in New York?

Medicaid.gov lists New York State Department of Health. Applications go to the state, not to this website.

Does New York use the standard Medigap letters?

Yes. Medicare.gov standardizes letters A, B, C, D, F, G, K, L, M, and N in this state. Prices still differ by company.

Can I apply for Medigap in New York after the six month federal window?

Yes. The Department of Financial Services says an insurer that sells Medigap must accept a Medicare enrollee's application throughout the year, and may not deny it or price it from health status. Group policies are limited to members of the group.

Is Medicare Select sold in New York?

Not at the moment. The Department of Financial Services page, read 20 Nov 2026, says no insurer is currently offering Medicare Select in New York State.
