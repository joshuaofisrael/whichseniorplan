Who to call in Kansas: Senior Health Insurance Counseling for Kansas (SHICK), 1-800-860-5260. For help paying premiums, KanCare runs the state Medicaid and Medicare Savings Programs. We do not list private plan names or premiums.

Medigap in this state

In Kansas, Medigap policies use the standardized letters Medicare.gov publishes for most states: A, B, C, D, F, G, K, L, M, and N. The same letter has the same basic benefits at every company. The premium is not standardized, and this page does not list premiums or company names.

We did not confirm a state insurance department homepage on this pass, so we are not guessing a web address. A SHIP counselor can point you to the office that regulates Medigap.

What is the same in every state

Part A and Part B rules, the 2026 federal premiums on our costs page, and the enrollment deadlines come from federal law. Advantage and drug plans that are actually sold here are local. Compare them on Medicare.gov or with the counseling program above. Read Original Medicare or Medicare Advantage before you pick a path.

Questions people ask

Who offers free Medicare counseling in Kansas?

Senior Health Insurance Counseling for Kansas (SHICK) is the program listed on shiphelp.org. The number on that page is 1-800-860-5260.

Where do I apply for a Medicare Savings Program in Kansas?

Medicaid.gov lists KanCare. Applications go to the state, not to this website.

Does Kansas use the standard Medigap letters?

Yes. Medicare.gov standardizes letters A, B, C, D, F, G, K, L, M, and N in this state. Prices still differ by company.
