Who to call in Ohio: Ohio Senior Health Insurance Information Program (OSHIIP), 1-800-686-1578. For help paying premiums, Ohio Department of Medicaid runs the state Medicaid and Medicare Savings Programs. We do not list private plan names or premiums.

Medigap in this state

In Ohio, Medigap policies use the standardized letters Medicare.gov publishes for most states: A, B, C, D, F, G, K, L, M, and N. The same letter has the same basic benefits at every company. The premium is not standardized, and this page does not list premiums or company names.

State insurance department: homepage we confirmed. Medigap companies are regulated there. We did not copy a rate sheet.

What is the same in every state

Part A and Part B rules, the 2026 federal premiums on our costs page, and the enrollment deadlines come from federal law. Advantage and drug plans that are actually sold here are local. Compare them on Medicare.gov or with the counseling program above. Read Original Medicare or Medicare Advantage before you pick a path.

Questions people ask

Who offers free Medicare counseling in Ohio?

Ohio Senior Health Insurance Information Program (OSHIIP) is the program listed on shiphelp.org. The number on that page is 1-800-686-1578.

Where do I apply for a Medicare Savings Program in Ohio?

Medicaid.gov lists Ohio Department of Medicaid. Applications go to the state, not to this website.

Does Ohio use the standard Medigap letters?

Yes. Medicare.gov standardizes letters A, B, C, D, F, G, K, L, M, and N in this state. Prices still differ by company.
