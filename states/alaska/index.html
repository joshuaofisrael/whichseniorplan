Medicare plan choices in Alaska

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Who to call in Alaska: Senior Health Insurance Program (SHIP), 1-800-478-6065. For help paying premiums, Alaska Medicaid runs the state Medicaid and Medicare Savings Programs. We do not list private plan names or premiums.

Medicaid and Medicare Savings Programs

Medicare Savings Programs help pay Medicare premiums and, for QMB, other Medicare cost sharing. The 2026 federal income limits are on our savings program guide. The state can use higher limits. Apply with the agency below, which is the row Medicaid.gov publishes.

Medigap in this state

In Alaska, Medigap policies use the standardized letters Medicare.gov publishes for most states: A, B, C, D, F, G, K, L, M, and N. The same letter has the same basic benefits at every company. The premium is not standardized, and this page does not list premiums or company names.

We did not confirm a state insurance department homepage on this pass, so we are not guessing a web address. A SHIP counselor can point you to the office that regulates Medigap.

What is the same in every state

Part A and Part B rules, the 2026 federal premiums on our costs page, and the enrollment deadlines come from federal law. Advantage and drug plans that are actually sold here are local. Compare them on Medicare.gov or with the counseling program above. Read Original Medicare or Medicare Advantage before you pick a path.

Questions people ask

Who offers free Medicare counseling in Alaska?

Senior Health Insurance Program (SHIP) is the program listed on shiphelp.org. The number on that page is 1-800-478-6065.

Where do I apply for a Medicare Savings Program in Alaska?

Medicaid.gov lists Alaska Medicaid. Applications go to the state, not to this website.

Does Alaska use the standard Medigap letters?

Yes. Medicare.gov standardizes letters A, B, C, D, F, G, K, L, M, and N in this state. Prices still differ by company.
