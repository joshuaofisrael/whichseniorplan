Medicare plan choices in Missouri

By Which Senior Plan. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

General information from public pages. This is not insurance advice, not a recommendation, and not a government site.

Who to call in Missouri: Missouri State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP), 1-800-390-3330. For help paying premiums, Missouri Department of Social Services runs the state Medicaid and Medicare Savings Programs. We do not list private plan names or premiums.

Medigap in this state

In Missouri, Medigap policies use the standardized letters Medicare.gov publishes for most states: A, B, C, D, F, G, K, L, M, and N. The same letter has the same basic benefits at every company. The premium is not standardized, and this page does not list premiums or company names.

We did not confirm a state insurance department homepage on this pass, so we are not guessing a web address. A SHIP counselor can point you to the office that regulates Medigap.

What is the same in every state

Part A and Part B rules, the 2026 federal premiums on our costs page, and the enrollment deadlines come from federal law. Advantage and drug plans that are actually sold here are local. Compare them on Medicare.gov or with the counseling program above. Read Original Medicare or Medicare Advantage before you pick a path.

Questions people ask

Who offers free Medicare counseling in Missouri?

Missouri State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) is the program listed on shiphelp.org. The number on that page is 1-800-390-3330.

Where do I apply for a Medicare Savings Program in Missouri?

Medicaid.gov lists Missouri Department of Social Services. Applications go to the state, not to this website.

Does Missouri use the standard Medigap letters?

Yes. Medicare.gov standardizes letters A, B, C, D, F, G, K, L, M, and N in this state. Prices still differ by company.
