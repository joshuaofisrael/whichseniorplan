What to include

Send the page address, the sentence that looks wrong, and a link to the Medicare.gov, CMS, shiphelp.org, or state page that shows the current fact. We update the last checked date when we re read the source.

This is not a quote form. Do not send your Medicare number, Social Security number, bank details, or medical records. We will not use your email to sell you a plan or to pass you to an agent.

There is no form on this page on purpose. A form that asks for a phone number would look like lead generation, and we do not do that.

Which Senior Plan explains senior health plan choices in plain English, using public federal and state pages. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. government or the federal Medicare program. We do not sell plans.
