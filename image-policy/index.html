Image policy

Last updated 19 Nov 2026.

What we use

The mark and the social image are simple graphics we drew for this site. They do not include a government seal, the Medicare name, or a picture of a person.

Some guides use a photorealistic thumbnail created with AI. Those photos are labeled AI generated and link back to this page. They do not show a real identifiable person, a government seal, or a Medicare logo.

What we do not use

We do not use the Medicare card art, the CMS logo, state seals, or photos of real people. Any AI generated thumbnail stays labeled.

Questions

Copyright concerns go through the copyright page or the contact page.

Which Senior Plan explains senior health plan choices in plain English, using public federal and state pages. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. government or the federal Medicare program. We do not sell plans.
