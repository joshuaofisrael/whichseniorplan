Privacy policy

Last updated 16 Nov 2026. Which Senior Plan is a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company.

What we collect

The site has no account, no quote form, and no shopping cart. If you email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com, we receive whatever you put in that email and we use it to reply. Do not include your Medicare number or medical records.

Analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics loads on every public page and counts page views. It does not use cookies and it does not track you across other sites. Google Analytics 4 loads only after you accept the consent banner. Consent Mode v2 starts with ad storage, ad user data, ad personalization, and analytics storage denied. If you decline, the Google tag is not requested. Cookie settings in the footer opens that choice again. Accepting stores the choice in local storage in this browser. The site does not set its own tracking cookie. Advertising storage stays denied either way.

Hosting

GitHub Pages hosts the files and may log IP addresses for security.

Advertising

There are no ads. If that changes, we will update this policy and the advertising disclosure first.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13.
